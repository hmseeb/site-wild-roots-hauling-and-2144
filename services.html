Straightforward pricing

How our pricing works

We price by the amount of space your items take up in the truck. You get a firm number before we start — and that's the number you pay.

Single Item

One couch, mattress, fridge or appliance. The fastest and most affordable option — often done in under half an hour.

Quarter to Half Load

A garage corner, a small room, or a modest yard pile. The most common size for household cleanups.

Three-Quarter to Full Load

Whole garages, large cleanouts and remodel debris. Best value per item when you've got a lot to clear.

Multi-Load Projects

Estate clearances and commercial jobs are quoted as a project after a walkthrough, with a discount on repeat loads.

What's always included

  • All labor, lifting and loading
  • Transport and disposal fees
  • Sweep-up of the cleared area
  • Donation and recycling sorting
  • A free, no-obligation estimate
  • No charge if you decline the quote

Extra labor may apply for very heavy materials (concrete, soil, roofing) or long carries from difficult access points. We'll always tell you about it before we start, never after.

Please note

A few things we can't haul

For safety and legal reasons there are materials we're not permitted to transport. If you're not sure about something, call and ask — we can usually point you to the right disposal facility.

  • Wet paint, solvents and motor oil
  • Asbestos and hazardous chemicals
  • Medical and biohazard waste
  • Ammunition, explosives and propane tanks
