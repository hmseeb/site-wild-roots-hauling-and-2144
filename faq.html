Items & disposal

What we take and where it goes

Just about anything non-hazardous that two people can carry: furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, household clutter, garage and attic contents, yard waste, fencing, construction and remodel debris, hot tubs, sheds, play sets, office furniture and store fixtures.

For safety and legal reasons we can't haul hazardous materials — wet paint, solvents, motor oil, pesticides, asbestos, medical or biohazard waste, ammunition, explosives or propane tanks. If you're unsure about an item, ask when you call. We'll tell you straight away and can usually point you to the right facility for it.

We sort as we load. Usable furniture, household goods and building materials go to donation; metal, appliances and electronics go to recycling; clean green waste goes to be composted. Only what's genuinely unusable ends up at the transfer station.
