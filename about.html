About Wild Roots Hauling

A local crew that treats your home like our own — honest prices, careful work, and a real effort to keep usable things out of the landfill.

Our story

Built on showing up and doing it right

Wild Roots Hauling and Junk Removal started with a simple observation: getting rid of a couch or clearing out a garage shouldn't be the hardest part of anyone's week. Renting a truck, finding help, figuring out where things can legally be dropped off — it adds up fast.

So we built the kind of hauling service we'd want to call ourselves. You get a real person on the phone, a firm price before anyone lifts a finger, and a crew that carries things out carefully instead of dragging them across your floors.

The "wild roots" part matters to us too. Most of what people call junk still has life in it. We sort as we load, so furniture, appliances, building materials, metal and electronics get a second home or a second life whenever they can.

Our values

How we work

Four things we won't compromise on, no matter how big or small the job is.

Honesty First

The quote you approve is the price you pay. If a job turns out smaller than expected, we say so and charge less.

Landfill Last

Donation and recycling come first. The transfer station is where things go only when there's no better option left.

Respect for People

Estate and downsizing jobs are often emotional. We work at your pace, with patience and zero judgment.

Reliability

We turn up when we said we would. If something changes on our end, you hear it from us right away.
